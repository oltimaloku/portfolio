## Project Overview

ChefAI is an innovative iOS application designed to revolutionize how people manage their kitchen inventory and discover new recipes. By leveraging computer vision and AI technologies, the app provides an intuitive way to track pantry items and generate personalized recipe suggestions.

## Key Features

### Inventory Management

Users can easily build their digital pantry through multiple input methods:

- **Barcode Scanning**: Quick product identification using the device camera
- **Photo Recognition**: Take a photo of items for automatic identification
- **Manual Entry**: Traditional input for items not recognized

### AI-Powered Recipe Generation

The core innovation of ChefAI lies in its intelligent recipe generation:

- Analyzes available ingredients in your pantry
- Generates personalized recipes based on what you have
- Considers dietary preferences and restrictions
- Suggests substitutions for missing ingredients

## Technical Implementation

Built entirely with SwiftUI, ChefAI showcases modern iOS development practices:

- **Clean Architecture**: Separation of concerns with well-defined layers
- **Computer Vision Integration**: Utilizing Apple's Vision framework
- **AI Integration**: Leveraging language models for recipe generation
- **Local Storage**: Efficient data persistence for pantry management

## Impact

ChefAI addresses a common problem many households face: food waste and meal planning. By providing visibility into available ingredients and suggesting creative ways to use them, the app helps users:

- Reduce food waste by using items before they expire
- Save money by cooking with what's available
- Discover new recipes and cooking techniques
- Streamline grocery shopping
