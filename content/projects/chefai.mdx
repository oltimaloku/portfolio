## Why I built it

ChefAI was a small project with one goal: learn how to put AI inside a real app rather than next to it. The idea was simple enough to finish and concrete enough to be useful. Point your phone at what's in your kitchen, and get something to cook from it.

## How it works

- **Build a pantry** by scanning product barcodes with the camera, photographing items for recognition with Apple's **Vision** framework, or adding them by hand.
- **Generate recipes** from what's actually in the pantry. The ingredient list is sent to a language model, which returns recipes that use what you have and suggests substitutes for what you don't.

The app is written in **SwiftUI**, with the pantry stored on the device.

## What I took from it

Most of the work was in the seams: turning noisy camera results into clean ingredient names, and shaping prompts so the model returned recipes the app could actually display. Those same problems, making model output dependable enough to build a product on, came back at a much larger scale in [Neuron](/projects/neuron/).
